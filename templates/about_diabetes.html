About Diabetes

Understanding symptoms, causes, prevention, and precautions.

Symptoms
  • Frequent urination (especially at night)
  • Increased thirst and hunger
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Blurred vision
  • Slow-healing sores or frequent infections
  • Tingling or numbness in hands/feet
Causes & Risk Factors
  • Family history of diabetes
  • Overweight or obesity (high BMI)
  • Physical inactivity
  • Unhealthy diet (high sugar/fat intake)
  • Age above 45 years
  • High blood pressure
  • Gestational diabetes during pregnancy
Prevention
  • Maintain a healthy body weight
  • Exercise regularly (at least 30 min/day)
  • Eat a balanced diet rich in fiber
  • Limit sugary and processed foods
  • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol
  • Get regular health checkups
  • Monitor blood glucose levels periodically
Precautions
  • Take prescribed medications on time
  • Monitor blood sugar daily if diabetic
  • Follow a diabetic-friendly meal plan
  • Keep feet clean and inspect for injuries
  • Stay hydrated and manage stress
  • Carry emergency glucose if on insulin
  • Consult a doctor before any major lifestyle change
Important: This information is for educational purposes only. Always seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment.
